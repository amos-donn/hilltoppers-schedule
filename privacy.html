Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026.

Hilltoppers Schedule shows the school's daily schedule and lets students share their class schedule with each other. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to remove it.

Who runs this

Hilltoppers Schedule is a student-run project, not an official school system. Questions, or requests to delete an account, go to [[CONTACT EMAIL]].

What we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account identifier, your name, and your email address. We store those to create and identify your account.
  • Profile. The display name you choose, your schedule settings (time format, grade, lunch wave), the courses you enter for each block, and whether your profile is public or private.
  • Sharing. The access grants you give or receive, the schedule requests you send, and the profile IDs involved.
  • Technical. A session cookie that keeps you signed in, and the standard request logs Cloudflare keeps to run the service.

What we do not collect

We do not read your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, contacts, or any other Google data. Sign-in only asks Google for your basic profile. We do not run advertising or third-party analytics, and we do not sell data to anyone.

Who can see what

  • Public profile. Your display name and your profile ID are listed in the app and can be found by other signed-in students. Your courses are not shown in that listing.
  • Private profile. Nothing about you is listed. You can still send and receive schedules; someone can only reach you if you give them your profile ID yourself.
  • Your courses. Shown only to students you have granted access to, whether your profile is public or private. You can revoke that access at any time, and it stops.
  • Access codes. An access code is what proves a student may see your schedule. Codes are checked by the app but are never displayed to you or to the other student, and they cannot be passed between students.
  • Your email address. Not shown to other students and not used to find you. Only the profile ID is used for that.

Where it is stored

Account, profile, course, and sharing data live in a Cloudflare D1 database. The app runs on Cloudflare Workers, and sign-in is handled by Google. Those providers process data on our behalf in order to run the service.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Schedule requests and access codes do not expire on their own, so remove them yourself if you no longer want them.

Deleting your account

The "Delete account" control in settings removes your profile and your courses, revokes every access grant you gave or held, and takes you off other students' lists. It happens right away and cannot be undone.

Students under 13

This app is for students at the school and is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use it. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete an account by writing to [[CONTACT EMAIL]].

Security

We work to keep this data safe, but no system is perfect. If you want to stay private, keep your profile private and share your profile ID only with people you choose.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Continuing to use the app means you accept the current version.
